A high-fidelity UI/UX design mockup of an interactive real estate masterplan web application dashboard. The entire map is rotated diagonally on a dark, desaturated gridded satellite imagery background. Inside the blue plot, crisp white text displays '86', '100.43 yd²', '83.97 m²', and 'East'. Clean, modern, minimalist SaaS tool aesthetic, sharp vector lines, dark mode theme.
